Squarely privacy policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Squarely is a site-survey app for interior designers and site managers. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: everything you record stays on your phone.

What Squarely stores, and where

When you survey a site, Squarely stores on your device:

  • project, room and wall details you type in (names, client name if you enter one, measurements, notes);
  • photos you take of walls with the camera;
  • furniture and joinery layouts you place;
  • drawings, reports and 3D models the app makes from these.

All of this stays in the app's private storage on your phone. Squarely has no account or sign-in, and does not send your projects, photos or measurements to us or to anyone else.

Camera

Squarely asks for camera access only so you can photograph walls while surveying. Photos are saved inside the app, not in your phone's gallery, and are not uploaded.

Sharing

Files leave your phone only when you choose to share them: when you tap Share on a report, drawing or 3D model, Android's share sheet sends the file to the app or person you pick. What happens to it then is up to them and their own policies.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No collection of your location, contacts or other phone data.

Deleting your data

Delete a project (⋮ beside it in the project list → Delete project) or a room (Delete room at the foot of the room) to remove it, with its measurements, photos and layouts, from your phone. Uninstalling Squarely removes everything it stored. Android's own backup is turned off for Squarely, so its data isn't copied to your Google account.

Children

Squarely is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds a feature that sends information off your phone (for example, optional AI help with photos or design renders), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the feature will say so where you use it.
